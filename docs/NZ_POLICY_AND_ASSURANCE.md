# New Zealand Policy and Assurance

NZ Ledger is New Zealand-first. It is open-source software, not legal, tax, accounting, financial, investment, or regulatory advice. Users remain responsible for their obligations and outputs. New Zealand requirements change, and production use requires validation appropriate to the entity and circumstances. No government, regulator, tax authority, accounting body, or other professional body approval is claimed. This disclaimer does not excuse weak engineering.

## Primary-source requirement

Changing New Zealand obligations are researched from current primary official sources. Secondary commentary may help locate or understand a topic but cannot establish a project rule. Every policy item links to its source, access date, effective period, applicability, and any uncertainty. The [source register](SOURCE_REGISTER.md) is an index, not professional advice.

## Fact, interpretation, and implementation

Policy packs separate:

1. **Fact:** the authoritative source and its effective wording or published requirement.
2. **Interpretation:** the project's explicit reading, assumptions, scope, and unresolved judgement.
3. **Implementation:** versioned calculations, mappings, evidence requirements, tests, and outputs.

One layer never silently substitutes for another.

## Effective-dated policy packs

A pack records jurisdiction, rule identifier, source authority, effective and superseded dates, applicability, implementation, required evidence, review status, golden examples, and package version. Changes create a new version. They do not silently alter a closed period, filing snapshot, approved report, or previously produced result.

## Golden examples and review

Golden examples cover ordinary cases, boundaries, rounding, corrections, effective-date transitions, missing evidence, and known ambiguity. Examples identify whether they demonstrate source fact, project interpretation, or implementation behaviour.

Review is proportionate to consequence:

- **Unreviewed:** captured for research only.
- **Source verified:** primary source and effective dates checked.
- **Interpretation proposed:** project interpretation is explicit but not accepted.
- **Domain reviewed:** reviewed by an appropriately experienced accounting or tax contributor.
- **Security reviewed:** integrity and substitution risks reviewed where applicable.
- **Accepted:** approved for a defined version and scope.
- **Superseded:** replaced prospectively while retained for history.

Professional review does not become a general endorsement or approval claim.

## Historical reproducibility

Every material output retains the policy-pack version, evidence set, recorded-time basis, effective-time basis, calculations, approvals, and generated artefact hash. The system distinguishes an as-filed or as-approved view from the current corrected view. Reopening and amendment are explicit actions with preserved history.

## Records, retention, and export

Original evidence, financial records, policy sources, interpretations, decisions, and filed or approved snapshots remain traceable through retention and legal-hold policy. The architecture supports applicable retention periods, accessible and usable records, authorised offshore-storage requirements, English and te reo Māori records where required, backup and restoration, report and return reconstruction, and authorised review. Export uses open, documented formats and retains provenance, identifiers, versions, and hashes. Retention rules require current primary sources and professional review before implementation.

## Privacy

Collect only information necessary for a defined purpose, classify it, isolate it by tenant and legal entity, limit access through roles and mandates, record use and disclosure, protect it against loss and misuse, and support personal-information access and correction, retention and deletion policy, export, and breach response. Overseas disclosure and processor controls are explicit. Production AI use requires a privacy impact assessment, approved model-provider allowlists, and controls that prevent provider training on customer data by default. AI processing is subject to the same authority, purpose, minimisation, and evidence requirements. Public issues, Discussions, examples, and test fixtures must not contain real private or confidential records.

## Policy and judgement registers

The future accounting policy register records entity choices, scope, effective dates, approvals, source or standard basis, and supersession. The judgement register records material estimates and interpretations, evidence, uncertainty, reviewer, approval, and reassessment date. Neither register permits silent retroactive change.
